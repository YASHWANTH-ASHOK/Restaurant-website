ABOUT US

Welcome to our culinary haven, where the rich, authentic flavors of the Middle East and Arabian Peninsula come alive. We are a premier dining destination dedicated to serving the finest Mandi, Arabian Al Faham and refreshing juices, all crafted to perfection.

Our mission is to bring you an unforgettable gastronomic journey through traditional recipes, modern culinary expertise, and the freshest ingredients.

Our Specialties

Mandi: The crown jewel of our kitchen. Experience the true essence of traditional Arabian cooking with our beautifully spiced, slow-cooked meat served over a bed of perfectly seasoned, aromatic long-grain basmati rice.

Juices & Mocktails: The perfect finish to a spicy meal. We offer a vibrant selection of freshly squeezed fruit juices, thick shakes, and refreshing mocktails designed to cool your palate and revitalize your senses.

Why Dine With Us?

Authentic Spices & Recipes: We import select spices and follow time-tested cooking methods to ensure the flavors on your plate are as authentic as they get.

Uncompromising Quality: From premium cuts of meat to farm-fresh produce for our juices, we never compromise on quality or hygiene.

Warm Hospitality: More than just a meal, we offer an experience. Whether you are dining in with family or grabbing a quick bite, our team is dedicated to making you feel at home.
